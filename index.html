How it works
step 01
Paste a Spotify link
Open any track on Spotify, copy its link, and paste it into Fetch. Works with any Spotify track link.
step 02
Track info is loaded
Fetch automatically reads the song title, artist, album and cover art — no account or login needed.
step 03
Best match found
Fetch finds the best available audio for your track entirely on its own — no input required.
step 04
Saved to your device
Your song lands as a high-quality MP3 with the cover art and song details already embedded.
Features
No account needed
Works entirely without any account or login. Just install and go — zero friction.
High-quality MP3
Every download is a high-quality MP3 with album art and song details already inside the file.
Download history
Keeps your last 10 songs with cover art in a clean history panel, right inside the app.
Auto first-run setup
Everything sets itself up the first time you launch. Nothing to install or configure manually.
Dynamic theming
The app extracts your track's album color and applies it as a live atmospheric background.
Lightweight & fast
Fetch is small, fast and light. It won't slow down your PC or hog your memory.
